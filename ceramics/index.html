ceramics

I started working with clay in January 2026. I like to throw forms that I find practical, so you will rarely find me making decorative pieces. 

My current focus is repeating the full cycle of throwing, trimming, and glazing.

You can very occasionally find me at makers fairs. I've also started documenting my crafts on ig (@baysea.c).
